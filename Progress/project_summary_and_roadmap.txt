1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & CORE PURPOSE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Hydroponic farming (growing crops in nutrient-rich water without soil) produces 
up to 3x higher yields while using 90% less water than traditional soil farming. 
However, it is biologically fragile and unforgiving:
  * Water chemistry drifts quickly: If pH leaves the optimal 5.5 to 6.5 window, 
    plants experience "nutrient lockout" and can starve within days.
  * Nutrient concentration (EC/TDS) fluctuates: Water evaporation causes nutrient 
    salts to concentrate, burning plant roots; dilution causes malnutrition.
  * Mechanical failures are fatal: Without soil to store moisture, plant roots 
    dry out and wither in hours if a pump clogs, burns out, or loses power.
  * Manual monitoring is labor-intensive: Growers currently walk around with 
    handheld tester pens multiple times a day to measure water and air parameters.

WHAT PURPOSE DOES HAT SERVE?
HAT (Hydroponics Automation & Telemetry) transforms high-risk, manual hydroponics 
into an intelligent, self-monitoring, cloud-connected autonomous system. It:
  1. Continuously tracks all vital environmental and water metrics 24/7.
  2. Automates pump actuation based on soil/substrate moisture and safety timers.
  3. Detects environmental anomalies and creates instant alerts.
  4. Scans for hardware downtime and detects power or Wi-Fi failures.
  5. Provides a cross-platform mobile and web application so growers can monitor 
     crop health and toggle equipment remotely from anywhere in the world.

2. DETAILED BREAKDOWN OF THE 4 ARCHITECTURAL PILLARS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

PILLAR 1: PHYSICAL HARDWARE & EDGE FIRMWARE (ESP32-S3)
------------------------------------------------------
Location: c:\HAT\firmware\src\main.cpp

* Microcontroller: ESP32-S3 DevKitC-1 programmed in C++ via PlatformIO.
* Active Sensor & Actuator Pinout:
  - pH Sensor (Analog on GPIO 6): Reads water acidity/alkalinity. Configured on 
    ADC1 with 10-sample noise filtering so it operates smoothly while Wi-Fi is active.
  - TDS / EC Sensor (Analog on GPIO 3): Measures dissolved electrical conductivity 
    and nutrient concentration (ADC1).
  - Substrate Moisture Sensor (Analog on GPIO 1): Monitors moisture saturation in 
    the grow medium (ADC1).
  - DHT22 Sensor (Digital on GPIO 4): Measures ambient air temperature and relative 
    humidity.
  - Ultrasonic HC-SR04 Sensor (GPIO 5 Trigger, GPIO 18 Echo): Real-time water level 
    depth measurement in the reservoir tank.
  - Flow Rate Sensor (Digital Interrupt on GPIO 19): High-speed pulse counter 
    measuring liters per minute and circulating water flow.
  - Power Relay (Digital Output on GPIO 2): Actuates high-voltage equipment like 
    the water pump or grow lighting.

* Edge Intelligence & Reliability Features:
  - Dual-Trigger Auto-Watering:
      * Condition A (Moisture): Turns pump ON when substrate moisture drops below 30%.
      * Condition B (Timer): Runs pump every 120 minutes for a 45-second cycle.
      * Includes a 5-minute anti-chattering cooldown between automated runs.
  - Remote App Override: Manual toggles from the mobile app take immediate priority 
    over automated cycles.
  - Offline Fault-Tolerance Buffer: If Wi-Fi or backend connectivity drops, the 
    ESP32 stores up to 10 full sensor readings in internal RAM and flushes them 
    to the cloud as soon as the network reconnects.
  - Serial & Boot Stability: Configured CDC-on-boot and UART handshake handling to 
    eliminate microcontroller boot freezing.


PILLAR 2: CLOUD BACKEND & INTELLIGENT PROCESSING (FastAPI / Python)
-------------------------------------------------------------------
Location: c:\HAT\backend\src\main.py

* High-Performance REST API:
  - POST /ingest: Ingests, validates, and stores incoming sensor JSON payloads.
  - GET  /health: Liveness probe for cloud infrastructure monitoring.
  - GET  /alerts: Filterable alerts endpoint (by system, severity, or acknowledgment).
  - PATCH /alerts/{id}/acknowledge: Idempotent alert acknowledgment endpoint.
  - GET  /devices/{id}/status: Real-time device online/offline calculation.
  - GET  /devices/{id}/relay-state: Fetches desired/active relay state.
  - PUT  /devices/{id}/relay-state: Sets desired relay state from mobile app.
  - GET  /devices/{id}/relay-command: Lightweight poll endpoint for microcontrollers.
  - GET  /devices/{id}/pump-history: Event audit trail of pump toggle events.
  - GET  /systems/{id}/telemetry/hourly: Pre-aggregated hourly downsampled sensor 
    readings for historical charting.

* Security & Device Authentication:
  - Uses SHA-256 one-way cryptographic hashing to authenticate incoming X-API-Key 
    headers against the database.
  - Deactivated or rogue hardware can be disabled with a single database flag 
    (is_active = false), returning HTTP 403 Forbidden.

* Real-Time Alert Engine (alert_service.py):
  - Evaluates incoming telemetry against safe biological thresholds.
  - Triggers CRITICAL alerts if pH falls outside 5.5 - 6.5.
  - Triggers WARNING alerts if water temperature falls outside 16°C - 26°C.

* Device Offline Watchdog (watchdog_service.py):
  - Background async service running every 60 seconds on the server.
  - Scans last_seen timestamps across all active registered devices.
  - If a device stops transmitting for more than 5 minutes, it automatically creates 
    a CRITICAL "Device Offline" alert in the database.
  - Features smart deduplication to avoid repetitive alert spamming.


PILLAR 3: DATABASE & REAL-TIME STREAMING (Supabase / PostgreSQL)
----------------------------------------------------------------
Location: c:\HAT\supabase\migrations\

* Multi-Tenant Relational Schema:
  - profiles: User accounts linked to authentication credentials.
  - hydroponic_systems: Independent grow systems/greenhouses for multi-system support.
  - system_members: Role-Based Access Control (Owner, Operator, Viewer).
  - devices: Registered hardware, hashed API keys, last-seen timestamps, and relay states.
  - sensor_readings: High-volume time-series storage for raw sensor snapshots.
  - alerts: Central notification repository with severity and acknowledgment status.
  - telemetry_hourly_rollups: Optimized SQL view that pre-calculates hourly averages 
    for fast chart loading without querying thousands of raw rows.

* Security & Realtime:
  - Row-Level Security (RLS): Ensures users and organizations can only access data 
    belonging to systems they explicitly own or operate.
  - Supabase Realtime (WebSockets): Pushes new telemetry snapshots straight to 
    connected client apps in sub-second time.


PILLAR 4: USER DASHBOARD APPLICATION (Flutter Mobile & Web)
-----------------------------------------------------------
Location: c:\HAT\client\lib\

* Framework & State Management: Flutter, Dart, Riverpod, GoRouter, and fl_chart.
* User Screens & Features:
  - Authentication Gate: Secure Login & Signup flows with Supabase Auth session listener.
  - Multi-System Selection: Automatic system context detection with fallback screens.
  - Live Monitoring Dashboard: Real-time sensor cards for pH, EC, water level, 
    air/water temp, humidity, moisture, and flow rate with live ONLINE/OFFLINE status.
  - Remote Equipment Control: Interactive switch allowing instant toggling of the pump.
  - Historical Analytics: Interactive multi-metric line charts with 24-hour views.
  - Pump Activity History: Log detailing exact times and reasons for pump activations.
  - Alerts Center: Unread count badge, severity filtering, and one-tap acknowledgment.
  - Settings: Profile information, active system management, and session logout.

4. COMMERCIALIZATION VALUE PROPOSITION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

HAT is designed as a hybrid Hardware-Enabled Software-as-a-Service (H-SaaS):

1. Physical Hardware Controller (One-Time Sale):
   - A turnkey, plug-and-play controller box with industrial sensors and power relays.
   - Replaces custom DIY assemblies that require electrical engineering know-how.

2. Cloud Software Subscription (Recurring SaaS):
   - Monthly or annual recurring revenue for cloud hosting, telemetry retention, 
     historical analytics, automated alerting, and multi-user team access.

TARGET MARKET SEGMENTS:
-----------------------
A. Commercial Vertical Farms & Greenhouse Operators:
   - High willingness to pay to eliminate the catastrophic financial risk of crop loss.
   - Require multi-bed management, pump uptime guarantees, and historical compliance.

B. Boutique & Organic Specialty Growers (Microgreens, Berries, Herbs):
   - Small-scale commercial growers who need automation without paying tens of 
     thousands of dollars for enterprise industrial SCADA systems.

C. Educational & Agricultural Research Institutions:
   - Universities and ag-tech schools requiring precise telemetry data export for 
     experiments and student training.

D. High-End Residential Hydroponic Hobbyists:
   - Consumers seeking a dependable "set-and-forget" automation system for indoor 
     vegetable or flower gardening.

5. STRATEGIC ROADMAP FOR COMMERCIALIZATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

PHASE A: HARDWARE PRODUCTIZATION (DVT & MANUFACTURING)
------------------------------------------------------
1. Custom PCB Design:
   - Transition from breadboard/prototyping wires to a single integrated Printed 
     Circuit Board (PCB).
   - Integrate ESP32-S3 module, industrial screw terminals, reverse-polarity 
     protection, onboard 12V-to-5V/3.3V DC buck converter, and flyback protection 
     for relays.
2. Industrial Enclosure:
   - Fabricate a custom, wall-mountable IP65 water-resistant enclosure with cable 
     glands to protect electronics from high humidity and splashing nutrient water.
3. In-App Calibration Wizard:
   - Build a guided 2-point calibration UI in the mobile app (Buffer 4.0 / 7.0 for pH, 
     1413 uS/cm for EC) so end-users can calibrate probes without touching code.

PHASE B: CLOUD, SCALE & INTELLIGENCE
------------------------------------
1. Over-The-Air (OTA) Firmware Updates:
   - Enable the ESP32 to receive encrypted firmware updates over Wi-Fi, allowing 
     seamless deployment of bug fixes and performance enhancements.
2. Push Notifications & SMS:
   - Integrate Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) and Twilio for instant mobile push 
     and SMS text notifications when critical thresholds are breached.
3. Automated Dosing Expansion:
   - Expand the single relay to a 4-channel peristaltic pump module to automate 
     active pH balancing (pH-Up / pH-Down) and nutrient dosing (Nutrient A / B).

PHASE C: BUSINESS, ONBOARDING & SAAS INFRASTRUCTURE
---------------------------------------------------
1. Consumer Bluetooth Provisioning (BLE):
   - Implement BLE Wi-Fi provisioning in the Flutter app so non-technical users 
     can connect their controller to their local Wi-Fi network in under 60 seconds.
2. Subscription Billing & Paywall:
   - Integrate Stripe/LemonSqueezy into the platform to support tiered plans:
     * Free Tier: Real-time telemetry, 24-hour historical data, 1 device.
     * Pro Tier: Unlimited historical data, push/SMS alerts, multi-system control.
     * Commercial Tier: Multi-user team roles, PDF compliance exports, priority SLA.
3. Regulatory & CE/FCC Certification:
   - Conduct pre-compliance testing for CE, FCC, and RoHS standards for consumer 
     and industrial electrical safety.
